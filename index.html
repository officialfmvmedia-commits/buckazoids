THE LORE

In 1991, the Bitcoin symbol appeared in a game called Space Quest IV.

Seventeen years before Bitcoin.

01 · THE CURRENCY
$

Since the very first Space Quest in 1986, the galaxy ran on one currency.

Buckazoids.

The money of Sierra On-Line's Space Quest series. You earned them, you lost them, you mostly died holding them.

02 · THE COIN
₿

1991. Space Quest IV. Look closely at the coin in your inventory.

A gold B, struck through with vertical lines.

Strikingly similar to the Bitcoin ₿. Drawn years before anyone needed the symbol.

03 · THE NUMBER
59

You started with 59 buckazoids.

Nobody asked why.

That's why there are 59 of everything on this BBS. Calls today: 59. Price of the game: 59.

04 · THE PAPER
¶

October 31, 2008. A whitepaper, signed Satoshi Nakamoto.

A coin, at last, with no bank behind it.

"Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System." Seventeen years after the coin showed up in a game.

05 · BLOCK 0
#0

January 3, 2009. Block 0.

The coin finally had a chain.

Inside the genesis block, a newspaper headline from that morning. Its first 50 coins can never be spent.

06 · THE REVIVAL
!

March 27, 2025. A Reddit post: "BTC in a game from 1991?"

The coin woke up.

The community kept digging. The files live at Project B59.

EMBEDDED IN BLOCK 0 · 03/JAN/2009
"Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks"
headline, The Times, 3 January 2009 — written into the very first block forever
07 · COINCIDENCE?

Nobody has ever proven a link between Space Quest and Bitcoin. Most old fans say the B just stands for buckazoid.

Maybe it does. Nobody can prove otherwise.
