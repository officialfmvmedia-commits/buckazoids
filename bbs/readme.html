README.TXT  ::  BUCKAZOIDS  ::  CHAPTER ONE: THE FIRST COIN
================================================================

HOW TO PLAY
  - Move the mouse to the top of the screen to open the ICON BAR.
  - Pick a verb (WALK, LOOK, HAND, TALK) or an inventory item,
    then click on the scene.
  - Right-click cycles through the verbs.
  - Or just start TYPING to use the parser:
        look at crate
        use crowbar on crate
        talk to clerk
  - F5 = save game     F7 = restore game
  - Save often. This is 1991. The game WILL kill you.

STORY
  You are Wendell Flux, Sanitation Technician Third Class on the
  waste-hauler S.S. MEMPOOL. Everyone else is gone. You have a mop,
  59 buckazoids, and a bad feeling.

HINTS (highlight to read)
  Engineering left their toolbox open. Crates don't open by
  themselves. Lockers don't either. Never touch anything that
  sparks. Do NOT enter the rift empty-handed. Exactly 59 buckazoids buys
  exactly one disk.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  640K RAM, VGA, a mouse, a browser from this century.
